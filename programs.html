What We Do

Our Programs

How we're advancing personalized treatment for ALS and neurodegenerative diseases.

Patient-Derived Disease Modeling

We generate neurons directly from each patient's cells to build personalized disease models. These models allow rapid testing of potential treatments tailored to each individual's unique disease mechanisms.

High-Throughput Personalized Drug Screening

Using automated high-throughput assays, we identify treatments tailored to individual disease profiles and genetic backgrounds. Our platform screens thousands of compounds to find the right match for each patient.

Patient Support

Once we identify personalized treatments, we work closely with patients and their care teams to help bring these therapies back to them. Our goal is to close the gap between laboratory discovery and patient care.
